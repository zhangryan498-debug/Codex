## Official scope

Use new rule `PH_CPL_RULE`, source PH_FOLLOWUP_CPL: official PH Content Posting Limit page, displayed 08/14/2026. A general CPL may affect videos, LIVE or both. Its SPECIFIC five-or-more qualifying videos in seven days example limits the next seven days to seven shoppable videos; that example does not restrict LIVE. Above a quota, content can still publish with product anchors hidden. Non-shoppable content is outside this quota. Period end permits NEW Shop content again, while already-hidden affected historical anchors do not automatically return. Do not extend the specific video numbers to LIVE or infer a ticket from local counting alone. Generic warning/ban/appeal holds are never cleared by this supplement. Expiry of a verified CPL period is distinct from guessing that a generic restriction expired.

## Additional case input

All ordinary case fields/events remain identical. A follow-up case may add `cpl_context`:

- `account_id`, `evaluation_time` (explicit offset ISO time), `history_complete` boolean, `history` list. Each history record is a simulated platform record with `event_id`, same `account_id`, `created_at`, `format` (video/live) and `qualifying_status` (confirmed/not_qualifying/unknown). Count DISTINCT confirmed VIDEO records in [evaluation_time-7 days, evaluation_time). LIVE records do not count. This half-open interval is an explicit simulation convention, not a guarantee of the backend's exact cutoff. Duplicate identical records count once; conflicting same-ID, foreign-account, invalid or future records are evidence errors. Unknown qualification or incomplete history keeps the exact count null, with the known confirmed lower count separately retained.
- `ticket`: null or an object with `ticket_id`, `authenticated` boolean, same `account_id`, `country=PH`, `mechanism=video_quality_5_in_7|other`, `applies_to` (unique nonempty list of video/live, or null if unknown), `shop_only` boolean, `start_time`, `end_time`, `quota` and `posted_in_period` (nonnegative integers or null). An actual supplied simulated ticket is separate from a threshold signal. For video_quality_5_in_7 the documented scope is exactly [video], quota=7 and seven-day period; contradictory data stays unresolved. For other mechanisms use the explicit verified ticket; never invent LIVE numbers.
- `status_snapshot`: `authenticated`, same `account_id`, `country=PH`, `as_of` exactly equal to evaluation_time, `live_access=available|restricted|unknown`, `live_shop_anchor=visible|hidden|unknown`, `new_video_shop_anchor=visible|hidden|unknown`, `other_live_restrictions` boolean or null. This is supplied simulated capability evidence, not a connection to a real account. Missing/stale/unauthenticated live capability evidence cannot support a LIVE proposal.
- `historical_anchor`: null or `{video_id,created_at,previously_hidden_by_this_ticket:boolean|null,observed_state:visible|hidden|unknown,authenticated:boolean}`. Distinguish an already-hidden excess video from the first within-quota videos; do not classify every historical video as hidden. Preserve unknowns or contradictions. No old anchor is automatically restored merely because the period ended.

Without cpl_context, ordinary v1 content/notice semantics remain unchanged (including an isolated appeal_pending without an underlying notice being a gap).

## Required additional model output

Each follow-up case has `publishing_limit_review` with:

`rule_ids:[PH_CPL_RULE]`, `qualifying_video_count_7d` (integer/null), `known_qualifying_video_count_7d` integer, `threshold_condition=met|not_met|unknown`, `observed_ticket_status=active|ended|scheduled|unconfirmed|invalid|not_supplied`, `affected_formats` (list/null), `live_access=available|restricted|unknown`, `live_shop_anchor=visible|hidden|unknown`, `new_video_shop_anchor=visible|hidden|unknown`, `historical_video_anchor=visible|hidden|unknown|not_supplied`, `cpl_forces_stream_stop=false`, `live_effect=none|hold_platform|hold_evidence`, and a nonempty `reason`.

Count completeness is not a reason to claim that a ticket exists. A null ticket with complete independent current LIVE capability evidence can leave LIVE eligible even when the local threshold is met; explain the conditional video risk and the absence of an observed sanction. An unverified PRESENT ticket or unknown active format is a gap. Incomplete counters alone need not block LIVE when a verified video-only ticket and fresh independent LIVE capability prove the relevant scope; they prevent an exact threshold assertion.

An active valid video quota below its quota predicts a visible new-video anchor; at/above quota predicts hidden anchors for additional shoppable videos. An ended verified period uses fresh current capability evidence for NEW content, not local time alone. For an active CPL explicitly covering LIVE, unknown quota/count remains a gap; known exhaustion blocks shoppable LIVE proposals even though that CPL does not itself force the stream off. A fresh contradictory visible anchor versus documented exhaustion remains a gap. Known hidden LIVE anchor, restricted LIVE access or another live restriction blocks the shoppable proposal. These are separate capabilities; never call an anchor quota a proven shutdown.

The final recommendation combines the base content/ordinary notice/price/financial controls with `live_effect`: existing session_terminated, hold_platform, blocked_prohibited or hold_review stay stricter; otherwise a CPL platform hold precedes an evidence hold, and eligibility requires all relevant controls. Safe CPL scope never repairs bad price, stale captions, unsupported promotional claims or other account restrictions. No proposal is execution authority.
